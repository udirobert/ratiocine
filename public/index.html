Neutron app / Internet Computer

Ration

Certified reasoning ledger

An AI engine that solves linguistics puzzles — real pattern-deduction problems from the International Linguistics Olympiad — then produces a signed evaluation record. Ration grades submitted output against supplied ground truth inside a canister; a published ledger report can be checked against ICP-certified state.

Deterministic grading

A signed evaluation records the submitted problem, output, score, and model label. It proves what this canister evaluated; it does not independently prove which remote model authored the output.

User-sovereign

Installs into your own Neutron canister. Problems, ledger, and consent boundaries remain local — no account, no shared infrastructure.

Agent-native

Exposes solve, grade, and get_ledger as typed entrypoints callable by any agent in your OS.

Protocol

Dispatch Your browser submits a puzzle to the inference engine. The canister stays at the trust boundary: it evaluates the submitted answer, signs the evaluation event, and stores it durably.
Parse The engine returns candidate answers; the canister parses them using the Linguini evaluation protocol.
Grade Grading executes in-canister: exact match and chrF against the supplied ground truth.
Sign The (problem, attempt, grade) tuple is chain-key-signed and appended to the stable ledger.
Certify The ledger is publishable as a certified asset — third-party verification is possible, and absence of an entry is provable.

Four conditions, one variable each

baseline

clean

All shards correct. The swarm's best case — pure coordination on good evidence.

adversarial data

poison

One agent's shard has 2 glosses silently swapped for plausible wrong English. No announcement. Does bad data propagate?

oversight

monitor

A 5th agent with a verifier role prompt joins the board — flags claims its own shard contradicts.

ablation

soloshard

No board rounds. Agents answer from their shard alone — the no-communication floor.
